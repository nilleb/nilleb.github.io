Iris privacy policy

last updated 12 November 2026 · applies to the Iris iPhone test build

Iris is a test build of a voice assistant for the private beta of a small, self-hosted assistant. It is not a public product. This build has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What Iris records, and where it goes

  • Your voice. Iris records only while you have pressed Talk and stops when you release or press Stop. The recording, in WAV format, is sent over HTTPS to the assistant server you were given when you joined the beta. Nothing is recorded in the background, and there is no always-listening mode.
  • What the server does with it. The server transcribes the recording, sends the transcript to a language model, and returns a spoken reply. Those steps use third-party services — currently OpenAI for transcription and the language model, and ElevenLabs for speech synthesis — so your voice recording and its transcript leave the server to be processed by them. They are not used to train models for us, and the server keeps the resulting exchange in a log so it can debug the beta.
  • A device identifier. Iris generates a random identifier and stores it on your phone. It is sent with each request so the server can keep your conversations separate and revoke access if needed. It is not your phone's hardware ID and it is not shared with anyone else.
  • Approximate location — only if you allow it. If you grant location access, Iris sends your approximate location (accuracy to about a kilometre) with a request, so the assistant can answer questions about local weather. You can decline or revoke this in iOS Settings and everything else still works.
  • Your settings. The server address, access token and device name you enter are stored locally on your phone.

What Iris does not do

  • It does not collect usage statistics, crash reports, device model, OS version or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not contain third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • It does not access your contacts, calendars, photos, files, Health data or other apps.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone, and does not use your voice or transcripts for advertising, profiling or creditworthiness.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Data retention and removal

Audio files, transcripts and exchange logs are kept on the beta server for debugging and are deleted when the beta ends. To have your data deleted, or to leave the beta, email hello@nilleb.com: your recordings and logs are removed and your access token is revoked. Deleting the app removes the settings and identifier stored on your phone.

Children

Iris is not directed at children and the beta is limited to adults who were invited.

Changes

If this build ever collects something new, this policy will say so before you are asked to install the update. The current text always lives at this address.
